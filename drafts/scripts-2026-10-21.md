## What I could and could not find

- **Arrive / Come back line text:** I could not find it. `report.md`, `report-2b.md` and `files-2b.tsv` hold durations and settings only, not the narrated words. I searched the repo history too, with no result. The only spoken text on record is `es-arrive-014`, "Gracias por regalarte este tiempo." (3.605 s, Jhenny 0.82). I reuse it as the Spanish closing line. The English equivalent is my own wording.
- **Style, taken from the clips:** Arrive has 23 EN clips and 15 ES clips. Come back has 8 clips per language. Most clips are one short sentence of 1.3–2.5 s, with a few longer ones of 4–7 s. I matched that: one idea per clip, short imperative sentences, second person.
- **Pause conventions:** none are written down anywhere in the repo. The pause lengths below are my proposal, not a copy of an existing rule. Short pauses of 3–5 s sit between instructions. 8–12 s is a breath or reflection. 15–30 s is silence.
- **Timing:** I counted words by hand and used the ~90 wpm EN and ~70 wpm ES you specified (speech time = words ÷ rate), plus the listed pauses. Measured clips would be better. Short Christian lines ran at about 110–130 wpm. Jhenny's one known line runs about 83 wpm. So real speech time may be shorter than my estimates, especially in English. Treat the totals as ±15%.
